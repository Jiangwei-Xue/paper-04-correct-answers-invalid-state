# Legacy Result Disposition

Older probes and matrices may explain why the current protocol was developed,
but they should not be pooled into the final primary matrix unless they satisfy
the current frozen controls.

## Allowed Uses

Legacy evidence may be used to:

- motivate the need for stricter state/control separation;
- explain why older SSR-positive evidence was not inherited directly;
- document method lineage and risk controls;
- justify diagnostic or ablation conditions.

## Disallowed Uses

Legacy evidence should not be used to:

- populate current primary rows;
- estimate the primary effect size;
- claim final method superiority under the current protocol;
- mix direct-provider and routed-provider results without a frozen route policy;
- bypass current task, prompt, scorer, and hash-lock requirements.
